The regional travel tab Pittsburgh does not have

Arriving students, families, and shift workers still bounce between FlyPittsburgh, TrueTime, and a rideshare quote. SteelLink compiles those sources into one operational picture and a grounded connection to the 28X Airport Flyer.

  • Problem: PIT and PRT already publish the data. Nobody compiles the last mile from gate to campus.
  • Wedge: Airport Flyer + Oakland/CMU/Pitt corridor, then expand to Amtrak and intercity buses.
  • Moat: Extraction + live GTFS-RT, not another wrapper around a chat model.
  • Next 48 hours: stop-level walk times, saved trips, SMS when a delayed flight should skip the current 28X.

SteelLink compiles public FlyPittsburgh boards and PRT developer feeds for Steelhacks 2026. Not an official ACAA or PRT product.
